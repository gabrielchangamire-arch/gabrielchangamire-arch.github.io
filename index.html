01 // About

I came to computer science because technology is one of the strongest levers we have for reducing suffering, and the systems that do that work have to stay up. That is what draws me to production engineering: understanding how Linux, networks, and services fail, and making recovery fast and boring.

My projects share one rule: every reliability claim is backed by evidence committed to the repo — load tests, failure drills, convergence measurements, and postmortems.

I completed the Meta-sponsored MLH Production Engineering Fellowship and am now a Software Engineering Fellow in MLH's Solana track. I also tutor CS at Bellevue College and am a Mentors in Tech fellow.
